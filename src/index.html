Welcome to SideQuest-OMORI

A tool to help complete your side quests in OMORI

With SideQuest-OMORI, you can travel the map of OMORI just like if you were on Google Maps. Use your mouse to drag and move around the map. Click on the red hands to change maps and go wherever you'd like. You can even use Pluto's Spaceline to travel faster to different areas of the game.

Or else, if you want to complete a specific quest, click on the "Open Quest Menu" button, and follow the steps to complete your quest in-game. The path will always be shown with green hands.

But first, you'll have to answer some questions to help the tool know your current progression

Warning! This tool is part of a school project. Currently, it does not contain all sections of the game nor all side quests. For a list of the side quests available, please refer to the quest list when you've completed the form!
